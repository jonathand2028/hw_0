My models.py (about 90 lines) is much simpler than ChatGPT's (about 570 lines).
I only included the classes and relationships that the assignment explicitly
asked for, while ChatGPT added extras like employee and student IDs, timestamps,
and additional validation. For the meeting schedule, I used separate days,
start_time, and end_time fields, while ChatGPT used a single meeting_schedule
text field. ChatGPT's version is more detailed and complete, and is likely
closer to what a real, fully usable database would look like, but it also
requires installing extra packages like Pillow for its ImageField (ChatGPT gave
install instructions after the code). ChatGPT's file is also almost double the
roughly 300-line guideline from class, so it would ideally be split into
multiple files anyway.
